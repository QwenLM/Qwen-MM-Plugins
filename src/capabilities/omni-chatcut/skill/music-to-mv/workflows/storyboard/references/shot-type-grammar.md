# Per-Shot Type Grammar

`shot_type` states the visible job of one editorial shot. It is independent of shot size and does not imply a global MV category.

## Common Contract

Every shot is self-contained and directly provides literal content, visible change, action, camera, music evidence, continuity, and transition.

## Dance

Movement itself is the content. Add `movement_design` with a specific style, movement quality, connected stages, musical accent actions, body channels, and any formation/camera protection.

## Narrative

A visible event, action, reaction, discovery, behavior, or result is the content. State exactly what is visible and how the shot connects to its neighbors. No objective/resistance/choice/cost formula, ordered global beat sheet, prop-count budget, or mandatory conventional story arc is imposed.

## Concept

A visual rule, observation, variation, scale/material change, or environment transformation is the content. Describe the visible change directly; symbolic interpretation is optional and is not an execution field.

## Performance

The singer or musician's delivery is the content. Add `performance_design` with delivery mode, visible gaze/breath/body/mouth/instrument behavior, and intensity change within the shot.

## Transitions

- `cut`: default visible boundary
- `dissolve`: overlap or time/space bleed
- `fade_in`: opening or deliberate reset
- `match_cut`: requires a concrete matched shape, position, motion, gaze, scale, or composition

Different shot types may follow each other whenever the action, image, music, gaze, shape, or space provides a clear handoff.
