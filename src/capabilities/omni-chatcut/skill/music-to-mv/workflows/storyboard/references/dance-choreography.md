# Dance Shot Design

Dance is a per-shot function. Put all information needed to generate the movement directly in that shot's `movement_design`:

- `dance_style`: specific genre or intentional hybrid
- `movement_quality`: groove, weight, attack, flow, suspension, resistance, isolation, or release
- `action_stages`: ordered connected movement across the full shot
- `accent_actions`: musical event plus visible body response
- `body_channels`: feet/weight, torso, arms, head/gaze, face, and spatial relation used in the shot
- `formation_start` and `formation_end` when multiple dancers appear
- `camera_protection`: body parts, formation, facing, or landing that must remain readable

Ordinary walking, unchanged posing, isolated arm waving, or a long held landing is not dance unless its groove and visual function are explicitly described. Remove fragile acrobatics, exact contact, or impossible unison when needed, but preserve connected movement and musical response.

Adjacent dance shots must agree on formation, facing, weight, travel direction, and action phase unless a visible transition changes them. Camera movement may reveal formation, follow travel, intensify an accent, or transfer focus; it cannot substitute for choreography.
